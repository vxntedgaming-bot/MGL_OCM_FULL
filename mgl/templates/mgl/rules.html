LEAGUE RULES

Ultimate Fantasy League is a real Career Mode. The website is the source of truth. Tokens, ownership and results only change after official server-side workflows.

Squads

Maximum squad size is 28. Starting squads are 25 players in the official positional structure, generated from UNASSIGNED FC26 players rated 64–69, and only go live after Owner approval.

Transfers

Managers can buy, sell, swap and request releases. There are no loans. Listings are capped at 5 active and 3 new listings every 24 hours. Agreements need league-office approval before ownership or Tokens move.

Auctions

Managers may submit 3 squad players every 24 hours. Durations are 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes. You cannot bid on your own auction. Unsold club players return to the original squad.

Scouting

One scout at a time. Choose country and position. The discovered player joins your squad when the scout returns, unless the squad is already at 28 — then the league office must resolve it.

Press

Each manager receives up to 4 questions every 24 hours. Approved answers pay 0.5 Tokens each, up to 2 Tokens per day.
