HOW UFL WORKS

  1. STEP 01

    Become a Manager

    Create an account and apply for a vacant club in Job Centre. Owner/Admin approval appoints you.

    Manager Career →
  2. STEP 02

    Build Your Squad

    Your official UFL squad is on the site. Mirror it in EA FC with Edit Team. Tactics stay yours.

    EA FC
  3. STEP 03

    Play Your Fixtures

    Arrange the match, play, then submit the result. The office approves and the table updates.

FAQ

How do I join?

Create an account, then apply for a vacant club in Job Centre. Signing up does not require approval.

When do I get a club?

Job applications stay pending until an Owner or Admin approves them. You are not assigned a club when you submit.
