HOW UFL WORKS

YOUR CAREER. YOUR CLUB. YOUR LEGACY.

UFL puts you in control. Manage your club, build your squad, play your fixtures and compete against real managers.

  1. STEP 01

    BECOME A MANAGER

    Create an account and apply for an available managerial position. Once approved, you receive your managerial position and enter Career Mode.

    MANAGER CAREER →
  2. STEP 02

    BUILD YOUR SQUAD

    Take control of your assigned squad and mirror your team inside EA FC. Manage players, formation, tactics and every important decision.

    EA FC →
  3. STEP 03

    PLAY YOUR FIXTURES

    Arrange fixtures with your opponent, play the match in EA FC and submit the result through UFL. Approved results update the competition automatically.

    FIXTURES →
  4. STEP 04

    TRANSFER & RECRUIT

    Manage your squad through the UFL transfer system. Make transfer requests, negotiate with other managers and use UFL Tokens where applicable.

    TRANSFERS →
  5. STEP 05

    BUILD YOUR LEGACY

    Results contribute to your league position, manager career and club history. Compete across seasons and become a UFL legend.

    CAREER MODE →

FAQ

How do I join?

Create an account, then apply for a vacant club in Job Centre. Signing up does not require approval.

When do I get a club?

Job applications stay pending until an Owner or Admin approves them. You are not assigned a club when you submit.
